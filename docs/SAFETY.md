# Safety notes

Live Quake Globe shows earthquake information. It is not a warning system.

* **It does not detect earthquakes or receive alerts.** The alert in the demo is a simulation. A
  button replays the M7.4 Hualien, Taiwan earthquake of 2024-04-03 from the USGS catalog, and every
  screen of it is labelled as a simulation.
* **Follow official guidance.** During shaking, drop, cover and hold on where you are; don't run to
  another room. The alert is designed around that: Drop, Cover, Hold On comes first, the cover spot
  you mark should be within a step of where you usually are, and nothing needs a gesture while the
  ground shakes. Official guidance: https://www.earthquakecountry.org/dropcoverholdon/ and
  https://www.ready.gov/earthquakes
* **No messages are sent.** "Sent to emergency contacts" is a simulated confirmation.
* **Quake data are preliminary.** The live globe uses the USGS real-time feeds, which update every
  minute; early locations and magnitudes are revised.
* **What a real version would need.** Phone alert texts don't carry the epicentre: Taiwan's
  earthquake alert gives the time and the region, and the US Wireless Emergency Alert for
  ShakeAlert gives no location. Precise epicentres arrive minutes later in official reports
  (Taiwan's Central Weather Administration, the USGS). Faster data would need a licensed
  early-warning feed (https://www.shakealert.org).
* **Glasses and attention.** Wear AR glasses only where a moment's distraction is safe, and never
  stop to put them on during shaking.
